Technology is shifting and many of us access the world differently. Social media no longer is a unifier, but it does not mean the social media-less must remain in the dark!

Introducing the Rudy-Smith Family Google Groups

Google Groups are a secure and easy way to share photos, life updates and news by simply emailing the group! Members of the group will automatically receive the email, directly to their inbox. From there, Reply goes privately to just the sender, and Reply All goes to everyone in the group. Groups also let family members upload and store photos and access them easily from Google Drive.

The Groups — Let's be honest, not every update is for everyone. The kids just wanna have fun.

  • The Rudy-Smith Family (family@rudysmithfamily.com) — All Rudy-Smith family members and spouses of all generations. The "general" list of everyone.
  • Rudy-Smith Boomers+ (boomers@rudysmithfamily.com) — Boomers and generations prior to boomers — you know who you are.
  • The Rudy-Smith Cousins (cousins@rudysmithfamily.com) — The next generation — the offspring of the Boomers!
  • Tech Support (techsupport@rudysmithfamily.com) — Probably just Addie and some crickets.

And the Rudy-Smith Family Drive

Alongside the groups, the family shares a Google Drive — a single place for photos, videos, reunion plans, and any documents worth keeping together. Everyone in the family groups can view and add to it, so the memories don't get lost in inboxes.
