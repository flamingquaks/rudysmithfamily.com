Groups

Email is the new town square — at least for this family. Here's who's in each group and when to send what.

How groups work

Each group below is just an email address. Send a message to the address and everyone in that group gets it in their inbox — like emailing one person, except it's twenty. Replies can go just to the sender, or back to the whole group. No app to install, no account to remember, no algorithm deciding whether someone sees your kid's first day of school.

  • The Rudy-Smith Family

    family@rudysmithfamily.com
    Who's in it
    Every Rudy-Smith and every spouse, every generation. The whole tree.
    Use it for
    The big stuff. Births, engagements, weddings, graduations, moves, milestone birthdays, reunion planning.
    Also great for
    Sharing photos and life updates — send 'em straight to the group, or drop them in the shared Drive folder and email a link.

  • Boomers & Beyond

    boomers@rudysmithfamily.com
    Who's in it
    Boomers and the generations before them. You know who you are.
    Use it for
    Anything aimed at the more finely aged end of the family — health updates, throwback stories, coordinating who's bringing what to Thanksgiving, sharing news the grandkids don't need a play-by-play of.

  • The Rudy-Smith Cousins

    cousins@rudysmithfamily.com
    Who's in it
    The next generation — the offspring of the Boomers.
    Use it for
    Whatever cousins talk about. Group trip plans, kid pics, wedding logistics, splitting a gift, planning the surprise.

  • Tech Support

    techsupport@rudysmithfamily.com
    Who's in it
    Probably just Addie. And some crickets.
    Use it for
    Login trouble. Not getting emails. Wrong group. Adding a new spouse, new baby, or new email address. Anything that isn't working right.
    Response time
    Best-effort, lovingly given.
